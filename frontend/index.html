What Is 2.5 mm of Rain?

Simple Everyday Guide

What does "2.5 mm" mean in real life? 2.5 mm is about half a teaspoon of water spread over a dinner plate — roughly a light passing drizzle that makes the road damp. If the weather app says 10 mm and 12 mm falls, the difference is only 2 mm, which is considered a successful, reliable forecast!

4 Things the AI Considers:
• Predicted Rain Amount (34% impact): Predicting light rain is easy; predicting massive cloudbursts is much harder.
• How Many Days Ahead (31% impact): Tomorrow's prediction is far more trustworthy than a forecast for next week.
• Location (35% impact): Coastal Chennai behaves differently from hill stations like Ooty or dry inland plains like Madurai.

🌊 Coastal Districts (Chennai, Cuddalore, Nagapattinam)

Right on the Bay of Bengal coast. Sea breezes and sudden coastal cloud shifts make rain timing harder to pinpoint. Weather models can occasionally over-predict light showers.

🌾 Interior Plains (Coimbatore, Tiruppur, Madurai, Salem)

Protected by the Western Ghats mountains. Because rain is less frequent, dry-spell predictions are consistently 90%+ reliable. Excellent for planning farming and outdoor work.

⛰️ Mountain Hills (Nilgiris, Ooty, Kodaikanal, Tenkasi)

Steep mountains push clouds upward, causing localized micro-climate showers. Weather can be sunny in one valley while raining just 5 km away, leading to higher forecast variance.
